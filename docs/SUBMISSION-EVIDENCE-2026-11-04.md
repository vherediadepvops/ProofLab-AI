## What the demo demonstrates

The [three-minute English demo](https://youtu.be/75i78EO8xZo) shows an evidence-based pendulum workflow: real recording → SAM object tracking → ProofLab motion measurements → Muse Spark interpretation → prediction frozen before second-video analysis → numerical contrast against a predeclared practical tolerance. The [live platform](https://prooflab-ai.com/) and [source/tests](https://github.com/vherediadepvops/ProofLab-AI) provide additional review paths.

The measured period is computed from video-based observations rather than fitted to the predicted period. A recorded practical-band outcome is not a proof of general scientific accuracy or a new physical law.

### Verified scope of the 4 November cohort

- The owner's “28 pendulum combinations” workbook has **25 pairing rows and 25 private recovery identifiers**. Its results cells still say “Pendiente”, but the saved production outcomes **were recovered** and are summarized per pairing in [Results](RESULTS.md).
- Read-only HTTPS GET verified **25/25 records with both videos, a frozen Muse prediction and a `COMPLETED` contrast evaluation**: **18 within ±5%**, **6 outside**, and **1 inconclusive**. No new model inferences were initiated.
- **Eight existing recordings** labelled 10–80 cm were reused. The three absent theoretical pairs were 10→60, 10→70 and 10→80 cm; the owner chose to work with these 25 registered cases only.
- The 50→60 cm recording pair incorrectly declared a 40 cm baseline (−11.23% outside band); 40→80 cm was inconclusive because follow-up SAM tracking coverage was only **3.07%**. Neither exception was silently corrected.
- Records were read through app endpoints backed by SQLite/files, **not** by independent raw database/provider-receipt audit. Agreement with a ±5% tolerance does not establish universal scientific accuracy or physical calibration.

Private recovery codes, nonpublic media, raw Meta responses, receipts and SQLite rows remain unpublished. The spreadsheet was not edited or published.

## Further scientific evidence needed

Independently measure effective length, pivot and perspective; quantify systematic errors; film a prospective follow-up only after freezing the prediction; and privately reconcile full hashes and provider receipts. These 25 records document real operation, including adverse outcomes, **not** 25 correct predictions or independently filmed trials.
