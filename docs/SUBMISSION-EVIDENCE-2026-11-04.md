## What the demo demonstrates

The [three-minute English demo](https://youtu.be/75i78EO8xZo) shows an evidence-based pendulum workflow: real recording → SAM object tracking → ProofLab motion measurements → Muse Spark interpretation → prediction frozen before second-video analysis → numerical contrast against a predeclared practical tolerance. The [live platform](https://prooflab-ai.com/) and [source/tests](https://github.com/vherediadepvops/ProofLab-AI) provide additional review paths.

The measured period is computed from video-based observations rather than fitted to the predicted period. A recorded practical-band outcome is not a proof of general scientific accuracy or a new physical law.

### Scope of the 4 November register

- Eight *existing* pendulum recordings are labelled by nominal string lengths **10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70 and 80 cm**; these labels are not independent calibration of effective length, angle, pivot or center of mass.
- The eight recordings permit \(\binom{8}{2}=28\) pairings mathematically. The spreadsheet currently contains **25** pair rows. The planned pairs **10→60 cm, 10→70 cm and 10→80 cm are absent**.
- All 25 observed-result rows retain the status **“Pendiente”**. Fields for measured T₁, the *actual frozen* T₂ prediction, measured T₂, and ProofLab verdict are **blank**.
- The spreadsheet's ideal periods are theoretical values from \(T=2\pi\sqrt{L/g}\) with assumed \(g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\); **they are not measurements from the recordings**.
- **The 25 identifiers have not been matched here to their immutable private reports or provider receipts.** No claim of independently verified completion or valid predicted-versus-observed agreement is made from these identifiers.
- These retrospective video pairings must **not** be presented as 25/28 independently filmed physical trials.

The register and original video files are not included in this public document. Original source files, per-case IDs, provider replies, receipts and SQLite records remain private until an authorized, redacted evidence release is possible.
